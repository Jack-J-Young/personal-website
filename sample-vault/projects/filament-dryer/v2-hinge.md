---
title: A hinge that does not fight the gasket
date: 2026-02-24
order: 2
---

The original lid was a lift-off, which meant every humidity check broke the seal and cost about
forty minutes of recovery. Adding a hinge sounds trivial and was not, because a hinge and a
compression gasket want opposite things: the hinge constrains one edge, and the gasket needs
even pressure on all four.

The version that works uses a printed living hinge on the long edge with the gasket stopping
short of it, and two toggle latches on the opposite edge doing the compression:

```
lid    ═══════════════════╗
gasket ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓     ║  ← hinge, no gasket under it
crate  ═══════════════════╝
       ▲
       toggle latches here
```

Humidity now settles at 11% overnight and holds at 13% with the lid opened once a day, against
20% for the lift-off version — most of which is the seal, not the hinge, but the hinge is why the
lid gets closed properly every time.
